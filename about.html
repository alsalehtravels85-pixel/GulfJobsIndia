ABOUT GULFJOBSINDIA

About Us

GulfJobsIndia is an independent online platform intended to help candidates explore job opportunities in Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain. The website provides vacancy details submitted by its operator and a simple way for interested candidates to submit an application.

Our purpose

We aim to present job information in a clear, mobile-friendly format so candidates can review the role, location, salary information, eligibility and application steps before contacting the listed recruitment contact.

Accuracy and verification

Vacancy information can change. Candidates should independently verify the employer, job offer, salary, visa conditions, recruitment authorization and any fees before making payments or travel arrangements. We aim to help candidates explore suitable vacancies and understand the stated role, salary information and application process. Final employment terms are provided in the employer's written offer.

Recruitment transparency

We intend to publish only opportunities we are permitted to share. The website should not be understood as claiming a recruitment licence or government affiliation unless that status is explicitly confirmed and documented.
