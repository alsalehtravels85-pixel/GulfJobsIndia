ABOUT GULFJOBSINDIA

About Us

GulfJobsIndia is an independent website that presents job information and application options for candidates interested in opportunities in Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain. The website is designed to make vacancy information easier to review on mobile and desktop devices.

What the website provides

Visitors can browse published vacancies, review available details such as role, location, salary information, qualification and experience, and use the application form where it is available. The website also provides application status information and contact options.

How to use job information

Read each listing carefully and confirm the employer, exact job duties, salary, contract, accommodation, transport, visa conditions, recruitment authorization and any charges directly with the responsible employer or appropriately authorized recruitment agency. Vacancy details may change and should be reconfirmed before travel or payment.

Accuracy and independence

GulfJobsIndia is not a government website and does not imply government endorsement. A listing on this website is not, by itself, proof that an employer or vacancy has been independently verified. The website does not guarantee selection, employment, work permits or visa approval.

Recruitment transparency

Recruitment activity and advertising of vacancies should be carried out only where permitted and in accordance with applicable Indian and destination-country requirements. The website does not claim a recruitment licence unless that status is explicitly confirmed and documented.
