Privacy Policy

GulfJobsIndia collects information that candidates submit through the application form, including name, phone number, optional email/message, and one PDF document. The information is used to review and respond to job applications.

Application documents are stored in private storage and are intended to be accessible only to authorized site administrators. Do not upload documents unrelated to your application. Access to the website or an application does not guarantee employment or a visa.

We may use an email service to notify the site operator of a new application. Do not submit an application if you do not agree to the use of your information for recruitment communication.

For privacy questions or document-removal requests, contact the website operator through the WhatsApp contact button on the homepage.

This is a starter policy and should be reviewed for the actual recruitment business and applicable laws before public launch.
