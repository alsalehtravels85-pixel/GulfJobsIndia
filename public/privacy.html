PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how GulfJobsIndia handles information when visitors browse the website or submit a job application. The website provides job information and an online application facility for opportunities in Gulf countries.

Information we collect

If you submit an application, the form may collect your full name, mobile number, optional email address, the job you selected, an optional message and one PDF document. Technical information may also be processed by the website host and security services to deliver and protect the website.

How information is used

Application information is used to identify the requested vacancy, review the application, contact the applicant about recruitment-related matters and administer the website. The website may send an application notification to its operator using an email service configured for that purpose.

Documents and storage

Uploaded PDF documents are stored in a private storage area and are intended to be reviewed only by authorized administrators for application handling. Do not upload documents that are unrelated to the application or that you are not authorized to share. No online service can promise absolute security, but access controls are used to restrict document access.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by service providers that host the website, store application records or files, protect the website, or deliver operator notifications. We do not display applicant phone numbers, email addresses or uploaded documents on public job pages. We do not sell application documents.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The website or its hosting and security providers may use essential cookies or similar technologies for operation, security and performance. If third-party analytics or advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize ads in accordance with their own policies and the choices available to visitors. This policy should be updated to identify the services actually enabled on the website.

Retention and requests

Application information is retained for as long as it is reasonably needed to handle recruitment enquiries, maintain operational records, address disputes or meet applicable legal requirements. To ask about access, correction or deletion of information you submitted, contact the operator through the WhatsApp link on the Contact Us page and provide enough detail to identify your request. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate operational reasons.

Children

This website and its application form are intended for people seeking employment and are not designed to collect information from children.

External websites

Job listings or messages may refer to third-party employers, agencies or websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Review those policies before sharing information with them.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when website features, service providers or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

For privacy questions, use the Contact Us page.
